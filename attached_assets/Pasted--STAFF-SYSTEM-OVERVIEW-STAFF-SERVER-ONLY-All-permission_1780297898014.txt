🧱 STAFF SYSTEM OVERVIEW (STAFF SERVER ONLY)

All permissions are controlled by role IDs in the staff server, not by Discord permissions.

Each staff rank is assigned a role ID:

* MOD → MOD_ROLE_ID
* TEAM LEAD → TEAM_LEAD_ROLE_ID
* ADMINISTRATION → ADMIN_ROLE_ID

⸻

⚙️ CORE RULE (GLOBAL HIERARCHY SYSTEM)

A staff member can ONLY use a command if their rank is HIGHER than the target user.

Rules:

* Higher rank → can moderate lower rank
* Same rank → ❌ blocked
* Lower rank → ❌ blocked
* Non-staff → can always be moderated

⸻

🧱 STAFF HIERARCHY ORDER

Highest → Lowest:

1. 🏢 ADMINISTRATION
2. ⭐ TEAM LEAD
3. 🛡️ MOD

⸻

🛡️ MOD ROLE (Basic Staff)

Permissions:

MOD handles basic moderation and user requests.

Commands:

* /unmute
* /ad-warn
* /messages
* /message-leaderboard
* /case-info

* /apply

* /break-request
* /break-end
* /current-breaks
* /ban-request
* /blacklist-request
* /network-ban-request
* /partnership-request
* /resign-request

Restrictions:

* Cannot use /jail or /unjail
* Cannot remove ad warns
* Cannot use /snipe
* Cannot manage staff
* Cannot use any /setup commands
* Cannot act on TEAM LEAD or ADMINISTRATION

⸻

⭐ TEAM LEAD ROLE (Advanced Staff)

Permissions:

TEAM LEAD supervises MODs and handles stronger moderation.

Commands:

* All MOD commands (inherit access)
* /jail
* /unjail
* /strike
* /strike-remove
* /promote
* /demote-user
* /manage-break
* /remove-ad-warn
* /reset-messages
* /reset-messages-all
* /snipe

Restrictions:

* Cannot ban users
* Cannot fire staff
* Cannot access /setup system
* Cannot use network admin commands
* Cannot moderate ADMINISTRATION
* Cannot moderate same-rank TEAM LEADs

⸻

🔇 MUTE / UNMUTE RULE (IMPORTANT DETAIL)

Rule:

A staff member can only mute or unmute users who are:

LOWER in the staff hierarchy

⸻

MOD:

* Can mute/unmute non-staff users
* Cannot mute TEAM LEAD or ADMINISTRATION
* Recommended: cannot mute other MODs

⸻

TEAM LEAD:

* Can mute/unmute MODs and below
* Cannot mute TEAM LEAD
* Cannot mute ADMINISTRATION

⸻

ADMINISTRATION:

* Can mute/unmute TEAM LEAD and MOD
* Cannot override owner (optional safety rule)

⸻

🧠 STRIKE / JAIL / STAFF ACTION RULES

* MOD → cannot jail or strike
* TEAM LEAD → can jail/strike MOD only
* ADMINISTRATION → can jail/strike TEAM LEAD and MOD
* Same-rank actions are always blocked

⸻

🧩 IMPORTANT SYSTEM RULES

1. Staff detection

* Only based on staff server role IDs
* Main server roles do NOT matter

2. Rank system

* ADMINISTRATION = 3
* TEAM LEAD = 2
* MOD = 1
* Non-staff = 0

3. Universal permission rule

executor rank must be greater than target rank
